Community Garden Handbook

Welcome to the Maple Street community garden. This handbook explains how plots are assigned, when the water is on, and what to do with the compost bins. Café volunteers keep the tool shed open from 8:00 to 11:00 on Saturdays, and the yearly fee is €25 per plot.

Plot Rules

  • Each household may rent one raised bed.
  • Beds left unplanted for six weeks return to the waiting list.
  • Keep paths clear of hoses and buckets.

Compost

Green waste goes in the left bin and woody stems go in the right bin. Turn the pile every second week; the volunteer rota lists who turns it.

What not to compost

Do not add meat, dairy, glossy paper or diseased plants. Weeds that have gone to seed belong in the green waste bag by the gate.
