# TORUS-C pre-registration: ray angle and polarity as the relation signature (written 2026-10-06, before any C solve, dev run or scoring)

Spec lineage: `torus_space_proposal.md`, TORUS-A/A2b (`torus_a_results.md`, `torus_a2b_results.md`), TORUS-B (`torus_b_prereg.md`, `torus_b_results.md`). All TORUS-C outputs go to `data/torus/c/`.

## Why this experiment exists
CEO directive 2026-10-06: "Torus C makes sense and ray tracing polarity angle." Thesis: the ray between two concepts' toroidal positions defines the relation. TORUS-B (negative result) found that unlabeled rays recover relatedness but not relation type: held-out NMI 0.128/0.131 (floor 0.15), popularity-matched AUC 0.759/0.742, relation-from-ray accuracy 0.07-0.08, composition 0.49x baseline. Diagnosis from B: (1) "related means near" sent 72% of edges to one near-zero ray; (2) rays encoded destination region, not relation; (3) direction was unused (orientation 0.51); (4) oracle-seeded labels reach only NMI 0.27 under translation geometry; (5) tori help modestly, tangent links not. TORUS-C gives the ray the structure it lacked: proximity and relation are separate subspaces, and the relation signature is the ray's ANGLE and POLARITY with magnitude removed.

## Questions
- C1 split subspaces: does moving "related means near" into a dedicated proximity subspace stop it absorbing the relation ray?
- C2 angle code and polarity: do per-T^2 direction sectors (magnitude removed) plus the half-plane (polarity) recover relation types and the head side of antisymmetric relations?
- C3 structural seeds: do label-free structural role signatures give the latent relations a start, and does the geometry then add anything over the seeds alone?
- C4 composed rays: do ray codes of two hops predict the two-hop fact?
- C5 which of: angle code, polarity, split subspaces, structural seeds, composed rays, multiple tori, the sphere's 64/256/1024 hierarchy as a coarse address, helped on dev and which did not.

## Data and the NEW split (fresh seed 6029)
- Facts: the 1,134,174 facts of `data/torus/a2b/a2b_data.npz` (13 relations, same sources: WordNet, ConceptNet, Wikidata; 10 scored: wn:hypernym, wn:part_holonym, wn:member_holonym, cn:UsedFor, cn:AtLocation, cn:CapableOf, wd:P19, wd:P17, wd:P131, wd:P171; auxiliary training-only wd:P27, wd:P20, wd:P106). Entities, names, origin tags and the lift pointers are unchanged. `torus_c_data.py` writes `data/torus/c/c_data.npz` with a new `split` column only.
- The A2b/B dev and test facts are never eligible for the new dev or test. Eligible fact: an A2b TRAINING fact of a scored relation, h != t, the unordered pair (h, t) is stored exactly once across all relations and orientations, both endpoints have training degree >= 3 at that moment (so >= 2 after removal), and t is a training tail of the same relation at least twice. Facts are visited in a seed-6029 random order; an eligible fact goes to whichever of test or dev has fewer facts of that relation (ties to test) while under the cap ceil(10%) of the relation's facts; everything else, including all old dev/test facts and auxiliary facts, is training. Achieved counts per relation are reported; short relations are not refilled.
- So every held-out entity has >= 2 training facts, no held-out pair appears in any other relation, and no A2b held-out pair is held out again. Disclosure: old A2b dev/test facts are training here.
- No-leak sphere rebuilt for the new split: `torus_c_data.py` writes the WordNet graph minus every new dev/test pair whose endpoints are sphere nodes, and the unmodified `scripts/concept_sphere.py` (CPU Taichi, paths redirected as in `sphere_noleak.py`) produces `data/torus/c/sphere_noleak.npz`. The live `sphere.npz` is never used. Sphere-graph "related" edges (`sphere_graph_cur/C.npz`) drop every new dev/test pair, as in B.
- Labels (13 relation ids) are used only to evaluate (NMI, residual NMI, label tables, composition chain choice, per-relation reports). They never enter seeds, placement, clustering or the density LUT; the label tables are output readouts built from train labels.

## Design (idea order; each is tried on dev only and kept only if it wins)
1. **Split magnitude and angle/polarity.** D = 64 digits per entity: proximity subspace dims [0, P), relation subspace dims [P, 64) with P = 32 (dev: 16/48 as ablation). The relation subspace is 16 tori T^2 of 2 dims each.
   - Magnitude (proximity): sum over proximity dims of the sharp circular-distance LUT of A2b between the two positions. Every training edge (and the neutral edges) pulls with ZERO ray in the proximity subspace, so relatedness lives there.
   - Angle: per T^2 of the relation subspace, the difference cell (dx, dy) in Z17^2 is mapped by a fixed 17x17 integer LUT to a direction sector: centred (cx, cy) in [-8, 8]^2, sector = floor(16 (atan2(cy, cx) + pi) / (2 pi)) mod 16, and sector 16 for (0, 0). The LUT is built once (atan2 is build-time; inference is a table read). Magnitude is discarded.
   - Polarity: antipodal rays differ by 8 sectors (ray(b,a) = -ray(a,b) flips the sector by +8); polarity bit = sector >= 8 (half-plane/handedness). A relation and its inverse share the sector axes (sector mod 8) and flip polarity.
   - Relation signature of an edge = its 16 sector codes. Its distance to a prototype code phi_k is the sum over the 16 T^2 of an integer circular-sector LUT (score 16/10/4/1/0 for sector distance 0/1/2/3/>=4; zero sector matches only zero sector).
2. **Placement (integer relaxation, no backprop).** Group A (dims [0, P)): all training edges, sphere-graph edges and sibling edges as zero-ray pulls with the A2b cavity term. Group R (dims [P, 64)): training edges with latent relation k, using the translation prototype c_k (per-dimension circular mode with the A2b cavity penalty, `fitT --tm trans`) as the consistent ray; neutral edges do not pull on group R unless an ablation switches them on. Latent relations come from hard EM over the relation-subspace translation score (E-step: best prototype; M-step: `fitT`; ICM sweep moves positions), never from labels. Start: no-leak sphere lift of the new split, warm-up identity sweeps as in B config R. The angle-code signature is the clusterer readout (`ang`: integer k-modes over sector codes with the sector LUT). A dev lever (`--ro ang`) replaces the translation fit in the relation subspace by a sector-LUT angle fit (magnitude-free objective) in E-step, M-step and ICM; it is built only if the translation version leaves room.
3. **Structural-role seeds (label-free).** From training edges only (no relation id, no neutral edges), per entity: origin tag o in {0,1,2}; in-degree bin (0 / 1-3 / 4-63 / >=64); out-degree bin (0 / 1 / 2-4 / >=5), the head's and tail's "hub" status being the top in-degree bin; hierarchy depth class = shortest directed stored-orientation path to a sink (a node with out-degree 0), capped at 3 (computed by 3 reverse-BFS rounds). Role = (o, in bin, out bin, depth class); the 24 most frequent roles (by train edge endpoints) are kept and every other role maps to the nearest kept role by Hamming distance over the four features (ties to the more frequent). Edge seed = (role(h), role(t)); the K most frequent role pairs are the seed clusters; every other pair joins the nearest seed pair by summed Hamming distance (ties to the larger). Seeds hold the assignment fixed for the first `seedfix` solver sweeps after the warm-up (dev: 0 / 10 / 20), then EM is free so geometry refines them. Seeded versus unseeded is reported on dev; the seed-only partition (no geometry) is a control on held-out edges (roles of the endpoints from the training graph).
4. **Composed rays.** Two-hop test on the relation subspace as in B6: predicted landing x_a + c_k1 + c_k2 (per-dimension, translation prototypes of the geometry-assigned clusters); angle codes add per T^2 through the vector sum (a pure angle code cannot be summed without magnitude, which is reported). Variants: stored hops (informational) and hop 1 held out (gate).
5. **Polarity test.** For the antisymmetric relations (wn:hypernym, wn:part_holonym, wn:member_holonym = part-of; wd:P131, wd:P19, wd:P17, wd:P171), orientation accuracy = share of held-out true edges (h, t) where the best-prototype relation-subspace fit of the forward ray beats the fit of the reversed ray (ties 0.5; chance 0.5).

## Metrics (all on the held-out facts of the 10 scored relations of the evaluated split)
- Everything of B (see `torus_b_prereg.md`): popularity-matched negatives (origin tag x degree window), degree-matched and uniform variants, popularity-only baselines, AUC with tie midranks, hits@10 (1 vs 99 matched), NMI/purity (held-out and train), relation inference with the majority table and the balanced table, the supervised naive-Bayes readout (diagnostic), composition, storage.
- Density LUT (P(related), no popularity term): cell = (best relation prototype or sector code k, 4 quantile buckets of the in-cluster relation fit, 4 quantile buckets of the proximity score); P = (n_pos + 1)/(n_pos + n_neg + 2) from 300,000 train positives and 300,000 popularity-matched train negatives, stored as an integer out of 288. AUCs of the proximity score alone and the relation fit alone are reported.
- Residual NMI against metadata: metadata cell M = (origin(h), origin(t), min(2, floor(log2(1 + deg)) // 3) of h, same of t), up to 81 cells (the B metadata clustering, training degrees). rNMI = I(C; L | M) / (0.5 (H(C | M) + H(L | M))) on held-out edges, which is 0 for the metadata clustering itself by construction. Within-stratum NMI (weighted by stratum size, strata with >= 200 edges) is reported next to it. Finite-sample bias of I(C; L | M) is read against the shuffled-position control.
- Strict relation inference (G3): per relation r the balanced table (cluster labelled by the relation whose edges are most concentrated in it, argmax_r n_kr / n_r over TRAIN edges) gives recall_r and precision_r on the held-out edges; relation r counts as inferred when recall_r >= 2 pi_r and precision_r >= 2 pi_r, with pi_r the train class prior (a predictor drawing labels from the prior has both equal to pi_r). The majority-table recall (the B read) is reported next to it.
- Seeds-only baseline: the structural seed partition applied to held-out edges, NMI, rNMI, recall (no geometry). Geometry "adds" if the headline clusterer beats it.
- Coarse address: sphere 64-cell pair (cell64(h), cell64(t)) clusters (entities with a sphere position; others get a "none" cell), top-K, as a clusterer and as an extra seed feature, dev ablation only.
- 10 largest unlabeled clusters (by train edges), each with 10 uniformly random member pairs, unfiltered; label mix per cluster; reported for seed 17.
- Controls (same code path): (S) shuffled positions within origin strata, both subspaces; (R) random rays; (M) metadata clustering; (E) seeds-only; (L) lift-only k-modes; (B) TORUS-B frozen config R re-solved on the new split with the B pipeline (`torus_b.py` semantics), for both seeds; (O) random orientation of training edges (dev). A supervised readout on the same rays is a diagnostic ceiling only.

## Gates (read on test, once per solver seed, for seeds 17 and 23; a gate passes overall only if it passes for both seeds; the numbers below are the minimums and are fixed before any C model result; Amendment 1 may only raise them, from dev controls, before the final solves)
- G1 relation recovery: held-out NMI >= 0.15 AND >= 3x the shuffled control AND residual NMI rNMI >= 0.03 (and >= 3x the shuffled control's rNMI).
- G2 relation-free link prediction: pooled popularity-matched AUC of P(related) >= 0.85 AND hits@10 (1 vs 99 matched) >= 0.30, with no popularity term.
- G3 relation inference: >= 6 of the 10 relations inferred under the strict balanced reading, and strictly more than the shuffled control's count.
- G4 composition, hop 1 held out: pooled hits@10 >= 2x the frequency baseline.
- G5 polarity: pooled orientation accuracy on the antisymmetric relations >= 0.70.
- G6 (geometry-adds, thesis reading): headline held-out NMI >= seeds-only NMI + 0.02 AND headline rNMI >= seeds-only rNMI + 0.01.
- TORUS-C passes only if G1-G5 pass on both seeds. The CEO thesis (the ray, via angle and polarity, defines the relation) is read as supported only if G1, G3, G5 and G6 pass on both seeds. A failed gate is reported failed with a diagnosis and the ceiling.

## Model selection (dev only, solver seed 17; every row goes in the ablation table)
- Reference on the new split: B config R re-solved (`--unlab 32 --tm trans --S sharp --lam 24 --rho 500 --warm 10 --em 2 --sweeps 50 --sib 1 --ge 1 --tan 0 --D 64`). Order: (1) subspace split P 32 unseeded; (2) structural seeds (seedfix 0/10/20, K 16/32/64); (3) angle-code clusterer readout versus EM prototypes, tori in the signature 4/8/16; (4) polarity / orientation read; (5) neutral edges on the relation subspace (sib, ge); (6) P 16/48; (7) sphere coarse address as clusterer and as seed feature; (8) composed rays; (9) angle-objective lever if built; (10) controls on the chosen model.
- Win rule: a setting is kept if it raises dev residual NMI by >= 0.01 without lowering dev matched AUC by more than 0.015, or raises matched AUC by >= 0.015 without lowering residual NMI by more than 0.01. Cost (digits) breaks ties. Seed noise from B (NMI +-0.005, matched AUC +-0.014) applies.
- No LUT is fitted on dev or test: the density LUT, the label tables and the seeds use training edges only, so the dev-mode solve is the test-mode training set.
- Final: freeze the chosen configuration, solve from scratch for seeds 17 and 23 on the training split, score each on test once (lock file), plus B config R on the same split for both seeds.

## Storage and PTEX
Digits per entity (positions D = 64), the prototype table (K x relation dims), the sector-code table, the density LUT, the label table. Packed with `torus_a l2pack` (adam-gf17 17-bit Reffelt pixel codec, 4096x4096 RGBA logical pages), roundtrip bit-exact; rust built with its own target dir, deleted afterwards. Reported against B's 64.005 digits per entity and the 3,264-digit cluster table.

## Paradigm declaration
Inference is integer only (digit differences mod 17, LUT reads, adds, argmax). Build-time floats are named in the results (atan2 sector LUT, sphere lift, histogram statistics rounded to integers before the ICM kernel, NMI/AUC evaluation). The scorer reads in-RAM int arrays; the pages are packed and verified. Nothing silently falls back to matmul or float weights; where something cannot be done it is reported.

## Amendment 1 (2026-10-06, written after the dev ablation and dev controls, before the final solves and before any test scoring)
Everything below was measured on the dev split of the new split (solver seed 17) and the training split. No test quantity has been computed. Dev numbers are in `docs/adam_t/torus_c_results.md` (dev ablation table); the lever order of the prereg was followed, with the additions named here.

### What dev showed (why the design changes)
- Translation prototypes (or the angle-sector objective) fitted to training edges in the relation subspace memorise the training edges and do not generalise: seeded or not, held-out NMI 0.04-0.10 (train NMI up to 0.37). Proximity subspace works: proximity score alone reaches matched AUC 0.83 (B config R: 0.784).
- What generalises is a relation subspace that carries NO training-edge constraint: only neutral second-order edges (sibling edges: heads sharing a tail, tails sharing a head; sphere-graph edges) pull entities of the same kind together (`--rnoe 1 --sibrel 1 --gerel 1`), and the relation signature is read afterwards as the ray between kind regions by an unsupervised clusterer (`m2` integer k-modes over translation rays, `a2` integer k-modes over angle-sector codes). Without any seeds this gives dev held-out NMI 0.26-0.30 and polarity 0.73 (vs 0.04-0.11 for fitted prototypes). Adding label-free structural role edges (entities of the same structural role pulled together in the relation subspace; roles from `torus_c_seed.py`, 48 roles) raises it to 0.41-0.51.
- The structural seeds themselves, with no geometry, read dev held-out NMI 0.43-0.45 (K 32-64) and residual NMI 0.38-0.39. Metadata (origin pair x 3x3 degree bins) reads 0.433. B config R re-solved on the new split reads 0.112 (residual 0.099), matched AUC 0.784.
- Angle-sector objective inside the solver (`--ro ang`) trains better (train NMI 0.31-0.37 vs 0.25) but generalises as badly as translation (held-out 0.05); the angle code is therefore kept as a clusterer readout (`a2`), not as a placement objective.

### Frozen configuration C* (chosen on dev by the rule below; table in the results doc)
- Placement: `--psub 32 --rstart 0 --unlab 32 --sweeps 50 --sib 1 --ge 1 --warm 0 --D 64 --rnoe 1 --sibrel 1 --gerel 1 --rolen 2 --rolef seed_K32r48.npz --lam 24 --rho 500 --lamr 9 --rhor 800` (proximity subspace 32 dims: all training, sibling and sphere edges as zero-ray pulls, lam 24 rho 500; relation subspace 32 dims = 16 tori T^2: sibling, sphere and role edges only, lam 9 rho 800; train-only solve; the `--unlab 32` prototypes are not used by the clusterer).
- Headline clusterer `m2`: K = 32 integer k-modes over the 32 relation dims (4 tori of 8 dims), `--lutk 0 --B 4 --Bp 16` (density cell = 4 relation-fit buckets x 16 proximity buckets, cluster-free). Angle-code clusterer `a2` (K = 32 sector-code k-modes, magnitude removed) is scored next to it on test as the CEO's signature. Both are frozen; the gates are read on `m2`.
- Composed rays: `--comp 1 --cprox 1` (landing = proximity coordinates of the head + summed relation prototypes; candidates = tails of the second-hop cluster).
- Selection rule used on dev (declared here after the fact for transparency): among configurations with polarity (antisymmetric, pooled) >= 0.70 and >= 6 strictly inferred relations, the highest residual NMI; matched AUC within 0.01 of the best. C* = row `k14` of the dev table. B config R is re-solved unchanged.
- Test scoring (once per solver seed 17 and 23, lock files): C* with `m2` and `a2`, the seeds-only baseline (seed_K32r48), metadata, controls (shuffled, random rays, lift-only), and B config R (`--clu m1 --lutk 1 --B 8 --Bp 1`) on the same split.
